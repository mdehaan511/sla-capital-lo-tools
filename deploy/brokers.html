Merge Duplicate-Email Brokers

Migrate inline broker data

Sweeps every loan, finds inline broker fields (name/email/phone) that haven't been linked to a Broker record, dedupes them, and stamps a brokerId back on the loan. After this runs, the Brokers admin view will correctly aggregate historical deal flow per broker.
