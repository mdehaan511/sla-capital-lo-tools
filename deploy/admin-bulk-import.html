Bulk Import Contacts

Upload a CSV of borrowers from your old CRM. We'll map columns, fuzzy-match owner LOs against Netlify Identity, dedupe against every existing client system-wide, and let you review before writing anything. Safe for large exports (tested up to 30 000 rows).

1Choose a CSV file

First row should be a header (Name, Email, Phone, Owner or similar). Delimiter auto-detected (comma or tab). Files ≤ 15 MB.

2Map columns

Pick which CSV column feeds each field. First 3 rows shown below for reference.

3Match owners

Each unique owner name from your CSV is fuzzy-matched to a live LO. Adjust any misroute before continuing. Unmatched rows must be assigned to an LO — nothing will be imported without a valid owner.

4Preview

Numbers you'll actually write once you confirm. Duplicates are skipped (existing records are left alone). Invalid rows are counted but not sent.
