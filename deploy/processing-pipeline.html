Processing Pipeline — Auto-Task Templates

Tasks listed here are automatically created on each loan the moment it enters the corresponding stage. Once per stage per loan — re-entering a stage doesn't pile on duplicates. Format per line: Title | days from stage. Days are optional (default 0 = no due date). Example: Order title insurance | 1 = create that task with due date 1 day after the loan hits this stage.
